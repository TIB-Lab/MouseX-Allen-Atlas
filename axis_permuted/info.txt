We provide a MouseX atlas in standard NIfTI orientation, together with an alternative version generated using a different axis convention commonly encountered in preclinical MRI processing pipelines.

Both versions contain identical anatomical information; however, they differ in their affine transformation due to a permutation of anatomical axes (notably a swap between coronal and axial dimensions). As a consequence, the two versions are expressed in different voxel-to-world coordinate mappings and may not directly overlay without appropriate reorientation.

Users should select the version that matches the orientation convention used in their preprocessing pipeline to ensure correct visualization and registration.
